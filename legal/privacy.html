1. Who we are

Pleroma is provided by Martín Rosa (Nitram), operating under the brand Carcamusa_Labs (“we”, “us”). Contact: carcamusa_labs@zohomail.eu. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

2. Local-first by design

Core world data (text, structure, and images you attach) is stored in a local database on your device. You can use Pleroma without creating an account (Free: one local world with full content inside, offline only). Local data stays on your device unless you export it or enable Premium cloud sync.

3. Data we process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account data — email and authentication credentials/tokens when you sign up or sign in for cloud features.
  • World sync data — world names, structure, custom fields, and (if you enable image sync) images you chose to push, stored under your account with our cloud provider.
  • Device permissions — access to your photo library when you pick reference or profile images (we do not upload your whole library; only images you select).
  • Diagnostics — basic technical logs our hosting/auth providers may generate (e.g. IP, timestamps) to operate and secure the service.

We do not currently sell personal data or use third-party advertising SDKs. If monetization (e.g. subscriptions or ads) is added later, we will update this policy and store declarations.

4. How we use data

We use data to: provide and improve the App; authenticate you; sync and restore worlds you choose to upload; prevent abuse and secure accounts; and comply with law. We do not use your world content to train public AI models.

5. Cloud providers / subprocessors

Optional cloud features use infrastructure such as Supabase (authentication, database, and file storage). Data may be processed in the region selected for the project (e.g. EU). Those providers act as processors for cloud features under their terms and DPAs.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Native iOS/Android builds do not use browser cookies. On the web version, we (and our auth provider) may use local storage / similar browser storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences. These are used for essential app function, not advertising tracking. We do not currently set non-essential analytics or ad cookies.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with processors that host auth/sync/storage, if required by law, or to protect rights and safety. We do not share your private worlds publicly.

8. Retention

Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Cloud account and synced worlds remain until you delete cloud copies, delete your account, or ask us to erase them. Provider backups may persist for a limited period.

9. Your rights & deletion

Subject to your local law (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data.

In-app: delete local worlds; remove individual cloud world rows; sign out; Delete Account (wipes cloud login, synced worlds, and Storage images). Local worlds on the device are not removed by Delete Account — delete those in the App if you want them gone.

You can also email carcamusa_labs@zohomail.eu with subject “Pleroma account deletion”. You can export worlds from the App for your own records.

10. Children

Pleroma is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard provider security (TLS in transit, access controls, row-level security on cloud tables). No method is 100% secure. Protect your device and password.

12. International transfers

If you use cloud sync, data may be processed outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by our providers.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, provide additional notice where appropriate.

14. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: carcamusa_labs@zohomail.eu
