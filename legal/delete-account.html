This page is for Pleroma by Carcamusa_Labs. Use it to request deletion of your app account and associated cloud data, including if you have already uninstalled the app.

What gets deleted

  • Your Pleroma account (email / login)
  • Synced cloud worlds and related cloud data
  • Synced images stored for your account in cloud storage

Local worlds that remain only on a device are not removed by account deletion. Delete those in the app or by uninstalling / clearing app data on that device.

Option A — In the app (if installed)

  1. Open Pleroma and sign in
  2. Go to Account
  3. Choose DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm

Option B — Request by email (web path)

Email carcamusa_labs@zohomail.eu with subject Pleroma account deletion.

Include:

  • The email address used for your Pleroma account
  • A clear request to delete the account and associated cloud data

We will process requests for the named account. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the email. After deletion, cloud account data is removed; provider backups may persist for a limited period as described in the Privacy Policy.

Partial data (without deleting the account)

You can delete individual local worlds and remove individual cloud world rows in the app without deleting your whole account.
